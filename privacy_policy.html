Privacy Policy for Scan My PDF

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Welcome to Scan My PDF ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application processes, stores, and handles your information.

1. 100% Local & On-Device Processing

Scan My PDF is built with privacy as our core fundamental principle. All document scanning, image filtering, optical processing, cropping, and PDF conversions happen 100% locally on your device.

  • Your scanned documents, images, and files are NEVER uploaded to external servers or cloud storage.
  • We do not operate backend servers that store or read your documents.

2. Permissions Requested & How They Are Used

To provide core document scanning functionalities, Scan My PDF requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely to allow you to photograph physical documents, receipts, book pages, and ID cards within the app. Camera data is processed exclusively on your device.
  • Storage / Media (android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to import existing photos from your gallery and save generated PDF documents and exported images to your phone's storage.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob / AdSense)

To support free access to our application, Scan My PDF uses Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC) to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), cookies, and approximate device data in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Scan My PDF does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
